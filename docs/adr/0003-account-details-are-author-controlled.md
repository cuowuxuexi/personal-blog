# Account details are author-controlled

Holdings, costs, orders, account size, and trade logs may be published at the author's discretion; agents do not treat them as private by default or silently remove them from public drafts. Credentials, private keys, personal secrets, private absolute paths, non-redistributable sources, and unpublished work materials remain excluded from public content.

This supersedes Decision 7 of ADR 0001. The research-authority boundary, publication gates, and prohibition on exposing private research materials remain unchanged.
